# Simple Interest Calculator

## Project Overview
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application that calculates the interest earned or payable on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate. It helps users understand how simple interest is calculated using a basic mathematical formula.

## Objective
The objective of this project is to develop a simple and easy-to-use calculator that computes simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula
Simple interest is calculated using the following formula:

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where:
- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money invested or borrowed.
- **Rate (R):** The annual rate of interest in percentage.
- **Time (T):** The duration in years.

The total amount is calculated as:

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## Features
- Calculates simple interest using the standard mathematical formula.
- Accepts principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period as inputs.
- Displays the calculated simple interest.
- Calculates the total amount, including interest.
- Simple and easy-to-understand implementation.

## Example Calculation

Suppose the following values are provided:

- Principal = ₹10,000
- Annual Interest Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

**Final Result:**
- Simple Interest: ₹1,000
- Total Amount: ₹11,000

## How to Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Calculate the simple interest using the formula provided.
5. View the simple interest and total amount.
